Interactive Timing Chart

Drag on the chart line to add an in-between; it snaps to the half or the thirds of the space it lands in. Drag a mark to move it; drag it well off the line sideways (or double-click it) to remove it. Placing a third also drops an ✕ at the other third: a spacing guide, not a drawing. Click an ✕ to make it a drawing, click again to turn it back.
